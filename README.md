
# ResQMeal 
### Rescue Surplus. Redistribute Smarter.

ResQMeal is a food-rescue and redistribution platform designed to connect surplus-food donors with people and organizations that can put that food to good use. By making surplus food easier to discover, manage, and allocate, ResQMeal aims to reduce avoidable food waste and encourage a more sustainable food ecosystem.

## The Problem

Every day, restaurants, hotels, and other food businesses may have surplus food that goes unused, while many individuals and communities continue to face food insecurity.

The challenge is not only food availability—it is also the lack of an efficient, coordinated way to identify surplus food, connect donors with recipients, and manage redistribution.

## Our Solution

ResQMeal aims to simplify the food-rescue process through a centralized digital platform.

The platform is designed to help:
- **Donors** list surplus food for potential redistribution.
- **Recipients** discover available food donations.
- **Administrators** oversee platform activity and support coordination.
- **RescueIQ** support intelligent matching and allocation of available donations.
- **RewardLoop** introduce a potential donor-reward system to encourage participation.

## Key Features

- **Food Donation Management:** Create and manage surplus-food donation listings.
- **Donor Dashboard:** Help donors manage their listings and track relevant activity.
- **Recipient Dashboard:** Provide recipients with a view of available donations.
- **Intelligent Matching — RescueIQ:** Aims to improve the matching of available food with recipient needs and allocation requirements.
- **Authentication:** Login and registration functionality.
- **Administrative Dashboard:** Support platform oversight and activity monitoring.
- **RewardLoop:** A proposed incentive concept involving rewards for eligible donors.
- **Food Safety Awareness:** Promote responsible handling and redistribution of surplus food.

*Feature availability and implementation status should be verified against the current working application.*

## How It Works

1. **List:** A donor adds details about available surplus food.
2. **Discover:** Recipients explore available donation listings.
3. **Match:** The platform supports matching donations with potential recipients.
4. **Coordinate:** Donors and recipients proceed with the redistribution process.
5. **Reduce Waste:** Suitable surplus food has an opportunity to reach people who can use it.
